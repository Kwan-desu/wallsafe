Privacy Policy for WallSafe

Effective Date: September 14, 2026  |  Zero Tracking

WallSafe is a privacy-first anime wallpaper manager and viewer designed with discretion, local-first architecture, and complete respect for user privacy. This document outlines how WallSafe operates and why it does not collect your personal data.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

WallSafe does not collect, record, transmit, sell, or profile your personal data.

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide any personal details to use WallSafe.
  • No Analytics or Ads: WallSafe contains zero third-party analytics SDKs, advertising beacons, or telemetry.
  • No Data Brokers: We never track your behavior or sell your information.

2. On-Device, Local-First Storage

All your activity in WallSafe is stored exclusively on your physical device:

  • Favorites & Collections: Saved in a secure on-device SQLite database.
  • Downloaded Wallpapers: Stored locally in your device's photo storage or application directory.
  • Preferences: Stored on-device via Android Jetpack DataStore.
  • Cache: Temporary image cache can be purged at any time in Settings.

3. Android Permissions Disclosed

Permission Purpose Handling
INTERNET Fetches wallpapers from public APIs. Direct encrypted HTTPS queries; no personal data sent.
SET_WALLPAPER Applies wallpapers to Home/Lock screen. Processed strictly on-device via Android WallpaperManager.
VIBRATE Provides haptic feedback on actions. Executed locally via VibratorManager.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Notifies user when wallpaper download finishes. Handled locally on-device via NotificationManager.

4. Children's Privacy

WallSafe does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). The app defaults to Safe for Work (SFW) content filtering.
