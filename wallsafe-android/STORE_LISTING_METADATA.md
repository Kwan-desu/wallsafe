## 2. Content Rating Questionnaire Guidelines
In Google Play Console → **Policy & Programs** → **Content rating**:
* **Category:** Utility, Productivity, Communication, or Other.
* **Does the app contain violence?** No (with SFW filters enabled).
* **Does the app contain sexuality or nudity?** No (App defaults to Safe/SFW rating).
* **Language:** No profanity.
* **Controlled Substances:** No.
* **Does the app allow users to interact with each other?** No (read-only client).
* **Does the app share user physical location?** No.
* **Resulting Rating:** Typically **PEGI 12** or **Teen** (suitable for anime art viewers with SFW filtering).

---

## 3. Target Audience
* **Target Age:** 13 and older (13–15, 16–17, 18+).
* Do not mark "Appeals to children under 13" to avoid COPPA Designed for Families requirements.
